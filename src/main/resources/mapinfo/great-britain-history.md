4500 BC - 42 AD:
- Various Celtic peoples arrive, leading to the adoption of agriculture and sedentary living. For this purpose, mass deforestation occurred.
- Stonehenge is built.

43 - 410:
- Julius Cesar attempts to invade Great Britain, and most of it becomes part of the Roman Empire. Later most of Wales and northern Scotland is also subjugated.  

411 - 864:
- The Angles and Saxons arrive, invading and immigrating into Great Britain forming Anglo-Saxon kingdoms. Kingdoms begin to be converted to Christians in mass.

865 - 877:
- Vikings from Scandinavia lead by the Danes arrive invading most of Great Britain.

878 - 1012:
- The remaining Anglo-Saxon Kingdom of Wessex managed to re-conquer Great Britain from the Vikings.

1013 - 1040:
- New Danish raids occur with the assistance of the Vikings, finally subjugating England to Denmark. 

1041:
- Edward the Confessor overthrows some of the danish rule in Wessex.
