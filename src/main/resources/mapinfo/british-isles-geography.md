# Climate
The British Isles have a temperate maritime climate.
# Geographical history
Whilst the isles used to be covered by forests, most of it was cut down for agricultural purposes.
This lead to the rise of moorlands in places of overgrazing.
Nowadays moorlands are the British Isles' defining biome.
# Biomes
- Moorlands
- Marshes
- Bogs
- Grasslands
- Forests
